# Sponsors

AgenTrust is an independent open-source project. No company funds, owns or directs it; contributions are voluntary.

OPAQUE Systems sponsored early development until October 2026.

## Recognition policy

Sponsorship does not confer project ownership, governance authority, or control over technical
decisions or demo results.

Sponsor recognition is separate from demo authorship and scenario provenance.
Vendor names and identities inside manifests, policies, fixtures, custody
roles, and deployment profiles describe the scenario being demonstrated; they
do not identify the owner of this repository or its projects.
